About

The Untaught is a not-for-profit (soon to be a community interest company) for people whose cultures don't often get a stage in UK music and arts.

That includes diaspora and global majority communities, LGBTQ+ people, and everyone who comes along to listen, dance, eat and take part.

We're starting close to home, with Indonesian and Southeast Asian diaspora culture in London and around. Over time we want to open the doors to more communities.

What we do (and will do)

  • Events mixing traditional and contemporary: dance, gamelan, live music, DJs and markets
  • Exhibitions, film screenings, talks and workshops
  • Paid work for underrepresented artists, and space for food and craft traders
  • Fundraising for our communities, here and back home, with tickets kept affordable

Why it matters

More visibility for cultures and artists that rarely make it onto mainstream stages, and real, paid opportunities for the people behind them. Welcoming spaces where you can reconnect with your heritage, meet others, and find belonging. Traditions shared with new audiences, and practical support when our communities are hit by crises like the Kalimantan fires.

Jalan Jalan

In Indonesian, jalan jalan means going for a walk, out for a wander. Ours is a night of Indonesian and Southeast Asian culture in London: gamelan and dance, DJs, food, and a market full of small traders.

Jalan Jalan #2

Our second edition is a fundraiser for grassroots groups responding to the fires in Kalimantan. Snacks and a vendor market, traditional dance, live gamelan, and a talk on Kalimantan's forests with a conservation author. From 9pm the DJs take over with Indonesian pop, disco, dangdut and breaks.
